Privacy Policy

Afrishine Hub — Testnet

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Afrishine Hub (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) handles information when you use the Afrishine Hub Testnet website and its features.

Testnet notice: This is a testing version of Afrishine Hub. Features may be incomplete, changed, reset, or unavailable. Do not submit passwords, financial account details, identity documents, or other sensitive information.

1. Information the test version may handle

The current test version is primarily a browser-based demonstration. Depending on the features you use, information may include:

  • Your Pi username when you choose to sign in through the Pi Browser/Pi SDK.
  • Information you type into forms or test features.
  • Basic technical information processed by your browser, hosting provider, or the Pi Platform, such as device, browser, and request information.

Some interface features are demonstrations only. Unless a feature clearly tells you otherwise, do not assume that information is securely saved to an Afrishine Hub account or database.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to operate and test the app, display the features you request, troubleshoot errors, improve usability, and evaluate compatibility with the Pi ecosystem.

3. Browser storage

The current front-end may use browser session storage or similar browser-side storage to keep temporary interface state, such as a signed-in display name. This information may be cleared when you close the browser or clear site data. It is not, by itself, proof of secure account authentication.

4. Pi Network and third-party services

If you use Pi sign-in or open this app through Pi Browser, the Pi Platform and its SDK may process information under their own terms and privacy practices. The website may also be hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process technical information needed to deliver the site. Please review the applicable policies of those providers.

5. Sharing

We do not claim to sell your personal information. Information may be processed by the platform and hosting providers needed to operate the test website, or disclosed where required by law. The current test version does not promise that every feature has a connected private backend.

6. Security

We take care when developing the app, but this test version is not intended for confidential or sensitive information. Do not use it to store passwords, private keys, wallet passphrases, identity documents, or banking information. Never share your Pi Wallet passphrase with anyone or enter it into Afrishine Hub.

7. Children

The test version is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact the app team so the matter can be reviewed.

8. Your choices

You can stop using the test website and clear its site data through your browser settings. If a future version provides account or data controls, those controls will be described in the applicable version of this policy.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app develops. The “Last updated” date above will be revised when changes are made. Please check this page periodically.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Afrishine Hub team using the official contact method published within the app. Before publishing, replace this instruction with a monitored support email or contact address.
